Why I started The Edge

I started the publication to write about epidemics that rarely get more than a few lines in a history book. I wanted to know what the people keeping the records could actually see. What did a burial register count? What could a physician infer from the patients who reached the ward?

I bring the same questions to contemporary outbreak reporting. With the exhibits, you can follow a ship’s route, compare archaeological sites, or open the source behind a point on a map.
