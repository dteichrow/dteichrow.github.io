Research profile

Epidemiologist and clinical research specialist with an MS in Epidemiology from UCLA and experience spanning neuroepidemiology, migraine, ADRD and cognitive aging, clinical trial data management, environmental epidemiology, health-services research, and public science communication. Brings applied quantitative analysis, protocol and IRB support, participant-facing research operations, manuscript and abstract writing, and clear translation of complex findings for clinical, research, and public audiences.
