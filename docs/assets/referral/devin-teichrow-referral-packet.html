Good first projects

The best referral is narrow: a site that needs launching, a paper that needs public-facing language, or a data/writing cleanup task that has been sitting around because nobody quite owns it.

Research visibility package

Data / stats / writing cleanup sprint

A fixed-scope cleanup sprint for analysis, figures, code, methods language, or results interpretation.
