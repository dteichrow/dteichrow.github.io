Behind the images

Documents, objects, evidence.

The exhibit covers draw on public collections and openly licensed research photographs. Here are the complete images, their creators, and the records that establish how they can be reused.

Wellcome Collection

Abstract of Quarantine Regulations

British government · 1826

Instructions for ships arriving in British waters. The cover introduces the maritime-quarantine dossier; it is a historical document, not current guidance.

Public Domain Mark. Wellcome image record rtwc97vd identifies this digitisation as public domain.

Library of Congress

Influenza ward, Walter Reed Hospital, Washington, D.C.

Harris & Ewing · c. 1918

A clinical scene from the 1918 influenza pandemic, one event within the much longer timeline.

No known restrictions. The Library of Congress states “No known restrictions on publication.” The catalogue dates the photograph c. 1918, Nov. 1, and cautions that its older caption-card data is unverified.

Library of Congress

Plan of Boston & vicinity

Cartographer unidentified · c. 1775

Geographic context for movement and encampments. The map does not itself record disease cases. North is to the left in the original.

Public domain. Eighteenth-century manuscript map. Library of Congress Geography and Map Division; the catalogue dates it [1775?] and the download is unrestricted.
