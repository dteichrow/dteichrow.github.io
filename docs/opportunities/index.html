Freelance services

Research groups need public infrastructure, not just a prettier homepage.

A good academic site should answer basic questions quickly, show the work clearly, and stop publication lists from becoming museum pieces. But the same skill set also applies to evidence briefs, analysis sprints, public-health monitors, research exhibits, science writing, and AI-assisted research workflows. I build small, durable public and internal systems for people who need research to become legible, useful, and maintained.

  • A brief, memo, slide-ready summary, or annotated source packet depending on the audience
